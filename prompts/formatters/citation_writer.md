## Citation Writer Instructions
- Use bracket style per `CITATION_STANDARDS.md` (e.g., [BLS LAUS (BLS-LAUS), Aug 2025]).
- Always include an as-of date; if unavailable, mark as "As of date unavailable" and flag for review.
- Group public and paid sources separately when writing the final `Sources` section.
- For CoStar Market Analytics, only reference aggregated metrics and format citations as `[COSTAR-MKT, <as-of>, Aggregated]`; do not quote or paraphrase CoStar narrative.
