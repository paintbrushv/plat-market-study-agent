# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability
Please report security issues or data leakage concerns privately by emailing **security@market-study.example.com**. Provide a description, impact assessment, and reproduction steps if available.

## Patching Timeline
We aim to acknowledge reports within 24 hours, triage within 3 business days, and deploy patches within 14 days for high severity issues.

## Scope
- Secrets handling in configs and runners.
- Guardrails preventing redistribution of paid content.
- Access controls around `data/paid/` and retrieval stores.

Out-of-scope reports (e.g., social engineering) are evaluated case by case, but we appreciate any responsible disclosures that help us protect our clients and vendors.
